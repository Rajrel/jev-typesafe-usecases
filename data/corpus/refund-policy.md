# Refund policy

Northwind Outfitters accepts returns for 30 days after the purchase date.

Duplicate charges are eligible for a refund of the extra charge. A delivered tent with broken poles is eligible when the request is inside 30 days. Leaking lids are eligible inside 30 days.

Orders older than 30 days are not eligible for a refund. The buyer may still ask for a paid repair.

Shipping fees are refunded only when Northwind caused the problem.
